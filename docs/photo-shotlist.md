# Product photography shot list

258 products have no photograph. 37 are branded stock whose supplier may already
hold usable images — ask before shooting those. The rest need photographing.

## How to shoot

A phone is fine. What matters is that every photo matches the others:

- One sheet of white card, same spot, same time of day. Daylight from a window,
  never the flash and never overhead room light.
- Fill the frame with the product, square on, a little space around the edge.
- Shoot square if your phone offers it; otherwise I will crop square.
- One product per photo. The name on the shelf label is the product.
- Send them to me in batches by category and I will crop, compress, size and
  wire each one to its SKU.

Tick items off as you go.
