Frequently asked questions

Do I need an account to order?

No. You can browse and check out as a guest. Signing in is optional and keeps your order history, addresses, wishlist and reviews together.

Can I order through WhatsApp?

Yes. Choose WhatsApp checkout to create a pending order and open a pre-filled message. DC Kids will confirm availability, delivery and payment with you in the chat.

How does direct Paystack payment work?

Paystack charges the products in your cart. Delivery is calculated separately, and DC Kids contacts you using the details submitted at checkout.

How do China pre-orders work?

Select a pre-order item or send us a photo on WhatsApp. We'll confirm the current closing date, deposit, estimated arrival time and balance before you commit.

Shipping and delivery

  • DC Kids delivers nationwide in Ghana through third-party riders and transport services.
  • Delivery is not included in the Paystack product total; the cost depends on your location and is confirmed separately.
  • Provide a reachable phone number, full address, city or area, region and a helpful landmark at checkout.
  • For an order update, use the tracking page or message us with your order number.

Track an existing order or ask about delivery on WhatsApp.

Returns and exchanges

Tell us on WhatsApp within 48 hours of delivery if an item is defective, damaged or different from what you ordered. Keep the item unworn, unwashed and in its original packaging while we review it.

Hygiene-sensitive, worn, washed or unpackaged items can't be returned unless they were defective. See the complete conditions in our Terms of Service.

Size guide

Available size labels and prices appear on each product. Children grow differently and supplier measurements vary, so age labels are a starting point, not a guaranteed fit.

  1. Compare the listed size with clothing or shoes that currently fit your child.
  2. When unsure between two sizes, send the product link and your child's measurements on WhatsApp.
  3. Confirm the selected size in your cart before sending the order or paying.
